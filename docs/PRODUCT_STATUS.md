# Product status

This is the canonical active maturity source. ShellForgeAI V1 is released, early beta-quality, guarded, and not production-autonomous.

## Released core and platform support

- **Linux/Docker is the supported V1 core** and primary release-validation lane.
- **Windows support is preview/early support**, validated for bounded local read-only evidence and selected Windows Server 2025 operator workflows.
- Unsupported operational routes fail closed before collection or model use.

## Available operator outcomes

The current product observes typed evidence, investigates and ranks likely causes, produces evidence-backed diagnosis and planning, synthesizes operator-ready recommendations, validates procedures and recovery guidance without executing them, and saves reports and handoffs.

Evidence-first routing and deterministic output do not require a model. When a provider is configured, model synthesis is grounded in the collected evidence and remains advisory.

Existing confirm-gated execute/remediation utilities are real bounded, governed compatibility/testing surfaces. They sit outside the primary recommended workflow and do not make ShellForgeAI production-autonomous.

## Contract ownership

Current maturity describes the product available today. [V1 Scope](v1-scope.md) owns the released contract, [Safety](safety.md) owns policy, and the [North Star](north-star.md) describes direction. Direction is not a claim that every approved solution is currently executable.
